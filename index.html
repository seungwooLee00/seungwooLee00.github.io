Seungwoo Lee

M.S. student, Sogang University · Software–Hardware Co-design

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Sogang University, advised by Prof. Youngmin Yi in the High-Performance AI Systems Lab.

My research is software–hardware co-design. A program runs fast only when its computation and data movement match what the hardware does well. I start from how a workload actually runs on a specific chip — where it waits on memory, leaves compute units idle, or runs out of on-chip storage — and reshape the kernels, the data layout, or the model to fit.

GPU and NPU kernels

  • Accelerating Sparse LU on GPUs (M.S. thesis, ongoing) — packs the many small dense blocks of sparse factorization into GPU warps and picks the kernel to match.
  • cuPF — batched power flow with the whole Newton–Raphson loop on the GPU; up to 27.94× faster than ExaPF.
  • Gemma-4 kernels on FuriosaAI RNGD — batch-1 decoder kernels restructured around weight streaming on an NPU; 8.05× kernel speedup.

Models for constrained devices

  • Early-exit NAS for microcontrollers — searches the network and its early exits under a 2 MB memory limit; 16.5% lower latency than MCUNet on an STM32H7. KCC 2025 Best Paper Award.
  • Quantization study (ongoing) — INT8 vision models on an STM32 and an RTX 3090; integer Softmax and row-by-row attention made MobileViT-XXS attention 1.41× faster with 35% less working memory on the STM32.

Teaching

  • Teaching assistant, Basic SoC Design (fall 2025) — built the course’s FPGA lab series on the Ultra96-V2, from adders to INT8 dot-product and GEMV accelerators driven by the ARM cores over AXI.

Other projects

  • Embedded System Software — Android app, JNI, a custom Linux system call, and a device driver on an i.MX6 board (course project).
  • Malicious URL and JavaScript detection — team lead and AI models; models quantized to INT8 for a CPU server. Minister of Science and ICT Award.
  • Michelin in TV — webOS TV app; frontend and testing (team project).
  • Reinforcement learning vs. genetic search for MCU NAS — graduate course project.
  • Compiler construction — course assignments in OCaml.
